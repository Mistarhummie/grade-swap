Questions parents ask

How much does GradeSwap cost?

Listing and searching are free. We take a small 10% fee from the seller when a book sells, so a KES 400 book pays the seller KES 360.

How does payment work?

The buyer pays GradeSwap through M-Pesa. We hold the money and pay the seller once the buyer confirms they have the books in the condition shown.

What if the book is the wrong edition?

Every listing includes the title, publisher, grade and photos. If you receive a book that doesn't match the listing, tell us before we release payment and you get your money back.

Which areas do you cover?

We're starting with a few Nairobi areas and schools, and growing fast. Send us your area and school, and we'll tell you what's available near you.

Do you buy books directly?

No. GradeSwap connects parents with each other. Your books go straight to another family who needs them.
